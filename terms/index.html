Benjamin: benjamin.scicoagent.com

What it does. Benjamin proofreads scientific documents (LaTeX projects, Word .docx, plain text, Markdown and RTF) and returns a corrected copy and a tracked-changes version. Corrections are presented as tracked suggestions for you to accept or reject. Paid plans add capabilities such as proofreading inline math. Features in development are marked as such on the product site.

Plans. The Free plan includes one document per month of up to 2,500 words. Paid plans (Pro and Max) have the monthly word allowances shown on the Benjamin pricing page.

Eratosthenes / SciCo-Search: search.scicoagent.com

What it does. SciCo-Search finds, ranks and analyses scientific literature for your queries. Its features include relevance scoring, author and topic views, and research-gap analysis. Relevance scores and gap analyses are computed estimates, not judgements of a paper's quality or correctness.

Plans. Plans, prices and limits are shown on the Search pricing page. On a team plan, the team owner pays for and manages the members' seats. Members can leave a team at any time.

Paper data. Bibliographic data comes from public scholarly sources, including OpenAlex. Coverage and metadata may be incomplete, and access to full texts is subject to each publisher's terms.

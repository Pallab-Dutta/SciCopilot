### SciCoauthor
- Write Thesis
  description: This is an adaptive Retrival Augmented Generation (aRAG) based LaTeX thesis writing assistant that converts published research papers into thesis-ready chapters while preserving scientific structure, citations, equations, and figure references. It is designed for researchers who already have papers, preprints, or publication assets in LaTeX and want to transform them into a coherent PhD thesis without manually rewriting every section from scratch.
